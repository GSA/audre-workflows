## audre-workflows

**AUDRE**—**Analyzing Usage, Depth, Resource Intensity, and Engagement**—is an open-source research toolkit for understanding how generative AI is used across organizations.

Basic activity measures such as users, prompts, and conversations can show that an AI platform is being used, but they reveal little about the work being supported, the intensity of an interaction, how activities relate over time, or where organizations should focus deeper evaluation. AUDRE brings these signals together by combining conversation-level task and topic labels, interaction depth, occupational work-activity mappings, estimated token and cost measures, organizational context, and directed transitions between temporally adjacent conversations.

The resulting analyses help organizations move from simple adoption counts toward a more contextualized understanding of enterprise GenAI activity. AUDRE can be used to identify recurring patterns, compare how AI use differs across organizational units, examine resource-intensive activities, and surface candidate AI-supported workflows for subsequent validation.

AUDRE is intended for descriptive measurement and evaluation prioritization. It does not treat platform telemetry as direct evidence of productivity, employee performance, task completion, cost savings, causality, or return on investment. The framework distinguishes between activity recorded by a platform, labels assigned analytically, relationships derived from those records, candidate workflows inferred from recurring patterns, and outcomes that must be measured independently.
